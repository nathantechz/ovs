The Nature of Vergence & Wavefront Propagation

Wavefront curvature, vergence formulas (L = 1/l, L' = L + F), dioptric power, and focal points.

Core Theoretical & Clinical Principles

Vergence Concept

Vergence measures the curvature of a wavefront. L = n / l (in meters). Diverging light is negative, converging is positive.

Thin Lens Formula

L' = L + F, where F is surface or lens power in Diopters (D).

Focal Lengths

Primary and secondary focal lengths: f = -n/F, f' = n'/F.

Clinical Pearl High prescriptions (> ±4.00 D) require vertex distance compensation to avoid under/over-correction.
Reference: Clinical Optics by Troy E. Fannin & Theodore Grosvenor (2018)
