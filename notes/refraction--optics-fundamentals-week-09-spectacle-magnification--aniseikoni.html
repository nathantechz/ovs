Spectacle Magnification & Aniseikonia

Shape factor, power factor, retinal image size differences, and Knapp's law.

Core Theoretical & Clinical Principles

Spectacle Magnification (SM)

SM = Shape Factor * Power Factor = [1 / (1 - (t/n)*F1)] * [1 / (1 - d*F_v')].

Aniseikonia

Perceived image size difference >3% causes stereopsis breakdown and asthenopia.

Knapp's Law

Axial ametropia corrected at the anterior focal point (d=15 mm) produces equal retinal image sizes.

Clinical Pearl Axial anisometropia is theoretically best corrected with spectacles; refractive anisometropia with contact lenses.
Reference: Clinical Optics by Troy E. Fannin & Theodore Grosvenor (2018)
