Corneal Microstructure & Transparency Maintenance

Epithelium, Bowman's layer, Stroma (collagen fibril lattice), Descemet's membrane, and Endothelial pump-leak mechanics.

Core Concepts & Clinical Theory

Five Classic Corneal Layers (Plus Dua's Layer)

Epithelium (regenerative, barrier), Bowman's (acellular, non-regenerating), Stroma (90% of thickness, uniform lamellae), Descemet's (basement membrane), Endothelium (metabolic pump).

Maurice Lattice Theory vs. Goldman-Benedek

Destructive interference of scattered light due to uniform spacing (< λ/2) of collagen fibrils maintains crystal transparency.

Endothelial Na+/K+ ATPase Pump

Maintains deturgescence (78% hydration). Cell density declines with age from ~3,500 cells/mm² in youth to a critical threshold of ~500 cells/mm² (causing bullous keratopathy).

Clinical Link Fuchs' endothelial dystrophy results in pump failure, stromal edema, subepithelial bullae, and morning blur.
Reference: Clinical Anatomy of the Visual System by Lee Ann Remington (2019)
