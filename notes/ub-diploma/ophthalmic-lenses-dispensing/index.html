Diploma in Optometry (2 years)

Ophthalmic Lenses and Spectacle Dispensing

From the optics of a spectacle lens to a finished, fitted pair — notation, prism and decentration, vertex distance, frames and lens mounting.

WCO competency scope Category 1 — Optical Technology Services. This is the course most fully inside diploma scope: every topic is something a dispensing optometrist performs directly.
