Diploma in Optometry (2 years)

Neurovisual Perception

How the retina and visual cortex turn light into sight — adaptation, acuity, contrast, colour and its deficiencies, spatial vision and depth.

WCO competency scope Category 2 — Visual Function Services. Measuring and interpreting visual function is the core of this course: acuity, contrast sensitivity and colour vision testing all sit squarely in diploma scope.
