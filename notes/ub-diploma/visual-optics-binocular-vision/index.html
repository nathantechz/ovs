Diploma in Optometry (2 years)

Visual Optics and Binocular Vision

The eye as an optical system, the optics of refractive error and its correction, accommodation, and how the two eyes work together — through to strabismus and fixation disparity.

WCO competency scope Category 2 — Visual Function Services, with Category 1 for the optical correction of refractive error and prism prescribing.
