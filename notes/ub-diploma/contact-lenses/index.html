Diploma in Optometry (2 years)

Contact Lenses

Ten topics from the first glass shells to modern soft lens optics — materials and manufacture, the tear lens that governs rigid lens power, care systems, and the complications that bring wearers back.

WCO competency scope Category 1 — Optical Technology Services for fitting, verification and care, with Category 3 for recognising and referring the complications of lens wear.

Grounding

Grounded in The IACLE Contact Lens Course, International Association of Contact Lens Educators, First Edition — Module 2 (Introduction), Module 3 (Fitting), Module 5 (Care and Maintenance), Module 6 (The Cornea in Contact Lens Wear) and Module 9 (Special Topics). The modules are First Edition and predate silicone hydrogels, so soft lens materials carry clearly marked current-practice notes.
