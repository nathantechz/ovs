Diploma in Optometry (2 years)

Optical Instrumentation

The instruments on a clinic bench — what each one measures, the principle it works on, how to use it, and how to read what it produces.

WCO competency scope Category 2 and 3. Operating and interpreting diagnostic instruments is a defining diploma competency; the electrophysiology topics are interpretive rather than operational at this level.
