Tscherning's Ellipse & Base Curve Selection

Derivation of Tscherning's ellipse from third-order aberration theory.

Core Theoretical & Clinical Principles

Wollaston vs. Ostwalt

Ostwalt branch provides flatter, cosmetically acceptable base curves.

Limitations

Applies only to spherical lenses gazing through center of rotation (27 mm).

Power Limits

Tscherning's ellipse closes above approximately +7.50 D and -22.00 D.

Clinical Pearl Plus lenses above +7.50 D require aspheric surfaces because standard spherical curves cannot eliminate oblique astigmatism.
Reference: Handbook of Optical Design by Daniel Malacara & Zacarias Malacara (2016)
