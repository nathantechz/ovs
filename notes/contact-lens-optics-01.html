Tear Lens Optics & Fluid Lens Calculation

The lacrimal lens created between RGP lenses and cornea, SAM-FAP rule, and spherical neutralization of corneal toricity.

Core Concepts & Clinical Theory

The Tear Lens (Lacrimal Lens)

When a rigid lens is fitted flatter than K, it creates a minus tear lens; fitted steeper than K, it creates a plus tear lens.

SAM-FAP Rule

Steeper Add Minus, Flatter Add Plus. For every 0.1 mm change in base curve (~0.50 D), adjust power by 0.50 D to maintain distance focus.

Neutralization of Corneal Astigmatism

Because tear index (n=1.336) closely matches corneal stroma (n=1.376), a spherical RGP neutralizes ~90% of corneal astigmatism.

Clinical Link Residual astigmatism in an RGP fit arises from internal lenticular toricity that cannot be neutralized by the spherical tear lens.
Reference: Contact Lens Practice by Nathan Efron / Phillips & Speedwell (2018)
