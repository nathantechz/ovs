Photoreceptor Metabolism & The 10 Retinal Layers

Outer segments, phototransduction cascade (rhodopsin, transducin, PDE, cGMP gating), and the metabolic functions of the Retinal Pigment Epithelium (RPE).

Core Concepts & Clinical Theory

Ten Histological Layers

RPE, Photoreceptor OS/IS, External Limiting Membrane, Outer Nuclear Layer, Outer Plexiform Layer, Inner Nuclear Layer, Inner Plexiform Layer, Ganglion Cell Layer, Nerve Fiber Layer, Internal Limiting Membrane.

RPE Critical Functions

Vitamin A cycle (all-trans to 11-cis retinal regeneration), outer segment disc phagocytosis, blood-retinal barrier (tight junctions), melanin light absorption, VEGF & PEDF secretion.

Foveal Architecture

Fovea centralis (1.5 mm), foveola (0.35 mm) contains exclusively cones, zero blue cones at center, high convergence ratio (1:1 cone to midget ganglion cell).

Clinical Link Drusen accumulation beneath the RPE (basal laminar deposits) impairs metabolic exchange, leading to dry and wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD).
Reference: Medical Retina: Focus on Retinal Imaging by F. Bandello & R. Silva (2020)
