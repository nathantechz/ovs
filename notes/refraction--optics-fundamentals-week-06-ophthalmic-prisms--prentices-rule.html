Ophthalmic Prisms & Prentice's Rule

Prism diopters (Δ), ray deviation, prism power calculation through decentration.

Core Theoretical & Clinical Principles

Prism Diopter (Δ)

One prism diopter deviates light by 1 cm at 1 meter: Δ = 100 * tan(θ).

Prentice's Rule

P = c * F, where c is decentration in cm and F is lens power in diopters.

Base Directions

Base-In (BI) for exophoria; Base-Out (BO) for esophoria; Base-Up/Down for vertical phorias.

Clinical Pearl Unwanted decentration in high-powered lenses produces significant induced prismatic strain and asthenopia.
Reference: Clinical Optics by Troy E. Fannin & Theodore Grosvenor (2018)
