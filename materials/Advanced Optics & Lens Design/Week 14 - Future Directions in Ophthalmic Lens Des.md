## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Liquid crystal tunable lenses, electro-active focus, wavefront-guided contact lenses.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Electro-Active Lenses
Liquid crystal alignment triggered by micro-currents to switch instantly between distance and near.

### Wavefront Customization
Correcting irregular corneal aberrations from keratoconus or corneal transplants.

### Smart Augmented Optics
Waveguide display integration for heads-up ophthalmic displays.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Wavefront-guided scleral lenses restore 20/20 visual acuity in severe irregular corneal disease where spectacles fail.
