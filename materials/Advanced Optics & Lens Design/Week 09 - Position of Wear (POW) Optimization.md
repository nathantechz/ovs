## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Compensating lens power for pantoscopic tilt, panoramic face form, and vertex distance.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Pantoscopic Tilt
Vertical tilt induces sphere and cylinder along horizontal axis: ΔF = F * tan²(α).

### Face Form Wrap
Horizontal wrap induces cylinder along vertical axis.

### Effective POW Correction
Recalculating fabricated Rx to match measured trial lens position.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** High wrap sport frames require POW compensation to prevent induced astigmatism and prism distortion.
