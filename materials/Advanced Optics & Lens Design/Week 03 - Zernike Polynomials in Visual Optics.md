## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Orthogonal Zernike expansion over unit circle, radial polynomials, and angular frequency.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Low-Order (LOA)
Piston, tip/tilt, defocus (Z2,0), astigmatism (Z2,±2)

### High-Order (HOA)
Trefoil (Z3,±3), coma (Z3,±1), spherical (Z4,0)

### RMS Error
Root-mean-square wavefront wavefront error in microns.
