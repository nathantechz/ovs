## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Conic sections (p-values, eccentricity), aspheric flattening, and atoric surface optimization.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Conic Equation
z = (c*r²) / [1 + √(1 - (1+k)*c²*r²)]

### Aspheric Benefits
Reduces lens thickness, weight, and magnification distortion in high plus/minus.

### Atoric Surfacing
Independently optimizing asphericity along each principal meridian.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Atoric designs give astigmatic patients uniform peripheral clarity across all gaze angles.
