## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Interference coatings, high/low index stacks, hydrophobic and oleophobic topcoats.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Destructive Interference
Optical path difference = (m + 1/2)λ between reflections.

### Multilayer Stacks
Alternating TiO2/SiO2 or ZrO2/SiO2 layers for broadband visible transmission (>99.5%).

### Hydrophobic Topcoats
Fluoropolymer sealing layers providing contact angle >110° for smudge resistance.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Modern hydrophobic coatings prevent water droplets and skin oils from filling micro-pores in the AR stack.
