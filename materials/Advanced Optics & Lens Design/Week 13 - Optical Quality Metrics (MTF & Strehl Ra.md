## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Modulation Transfer Function, spatial frequency (cycles/degree), Point Spread Function (PSF).

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Modulation Transfer (MTF)
Ratio of image contrast to object contrast across spatial frequencies.

### Cutoff Frequency
Diffraction-limited optical cutoff: f_c = D / λ (in cycles/radian).

### Strehl Ratio
Ratio of peak aberrated PSF intensity to diffraction-limited PSF (>0.8 is diffraction-limited).
