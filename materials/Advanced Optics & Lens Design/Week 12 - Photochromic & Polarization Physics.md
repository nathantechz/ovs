## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Silver halide crystals, organic naphthopyrans, dichroic iodine crystal alignment.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Photochromic Reaction
UV light breaks chemical bonds in naphthopyrans, shifting molecular conformation to absorb visible light.

### Temperature Dependence
Photochromics darken deeper and clear slower in cold temperatures.

### Polarization Efficiency
Stretched PVA film embedded with iodine crystals absorbing parallel electric fields.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Standard photochromic lenses do not darken behind automobile windshields because automotive glass blocks UV light.
