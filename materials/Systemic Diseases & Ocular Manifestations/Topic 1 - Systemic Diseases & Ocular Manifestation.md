## Overview & Objectives
Systemic Diseases & Ocular Manifestations: Etiology, cellular mechanisms, presenting symptoms, and characteristic biomicroscopic signs.

## Core Concepts & Mechanisms

### Etiological Classification
Infectious, autoimmune, degenerative, vascular, and genetic ocular pathologies.

### Biomicroscopic Signs
Endothelial precipitates, stromal infiltrates, microaneurysms, and intraretinal exudates.

### Differential Diagnosis
Distinguishing benign from sight-threatening conditions with similar clinical presentations.
