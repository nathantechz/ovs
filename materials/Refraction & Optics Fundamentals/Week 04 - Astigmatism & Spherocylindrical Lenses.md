## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Principal meridians, power cross, transpose calculations, and Jackson Cross-Cylinder optics.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Spherocylindrical Notation
Minus cylinder form (Sphere Cyl x Axis) vs. plus cylinder form.

### Power Cross
Plotting refractive power 90 degrees away from the cylinder axis.

### Transposition Algorithm
New Sphere = Sphere + Cyl; New Cyl = -Cyl; Axis = Axis ± 90°.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Proper axis alignment is critical: 5 degrees of axis misalignment induces ~17% uncorrected cylinder blur.
