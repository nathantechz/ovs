## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Chromatic aberration, Abbe value, Seidel monochromatic aberrations, and Tscherning's ellipse.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Chromatic Aberration
Transverse chromatic aberration TCA = (c * F) / V, where V is Abbe value.

### Abbe Values
Crown glass (59), CR-39 (58), Polycarbonate (30), Trivex (45), High Index 1.67 (32).

### Tscherning's Ellipse
Defines the ideal base curve (Ostwalt branch) to eliminate oblique astigmatism.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Polycarbonate lenses have low Abbe value (30); sensitive high-Rx patients often complain of chromatic color fringing.
