## Overview & Lecture Objectives
Objective retinoscopy, subjective refinement, Jackson cross-cylinder, binocular balance, and prescribing rules.

## Core Clinical Concepts & Mechanism Theory

### Subjective Refinement
Initial fogging (pushing plus), red-green duochrome balance, JCC power and axis refinement.

### Binocular Balancing
Prism-dissociated blur balance (3Δ BD OD / 3Δ BU OS) or Polaroid vectographic balance.

### Prescribing Pearls
Never change a comfortable cylinder axis arbitrarily; consider patient habitual wearing habits.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Pearl:** Accurate subjective refinement prevents unnecessary spectacle remakes and ensures high patient satisfaction.
