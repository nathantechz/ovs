## Overview & Objectives
Imbert-Fick law, standard 3.06 mm applanation diameter, central corneal thickness (CCT) compensation, and calibration.

## Core Concepts & Mechanisms

### Imbert-Fick Principle
W = P * A (Pressure = Force / Area). At 3.06 mm diameter (area 7.354 mm²), surface tension of tear film exactly balances corneal bending resistance (0.1 g = 1 mmHg).

### Fluorescein Semicircle Alignment
Correct endpoint is when the inner margins of the upper and lower fluorescent semicircles just touch at the midpoint of pulsation.

### Sources of Error
Thick cornea (>555 μm) overestimates IOP; thin cornea underestimates. Astigmatism > 3.00 D requires prism alignment at 43° to cylinder axis.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Link:** Post-LASIK patients have artificially thinned corneas, giving false-low IOP readings that can mask steroid-induced or open-angle glaucoma.
