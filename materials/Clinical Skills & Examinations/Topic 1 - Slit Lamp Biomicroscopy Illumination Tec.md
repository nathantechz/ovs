## Overview & Objectives
Direct diffuse, focal parallelepiped, optical section, specular reflection, retroillumination, and sclerotic scatter.

## Core Concepts & Mechanisms

### Parallelepiped vs. Optical Section
Parallelepiped (1-2 mm width) evaluates 3D corneal depth; optical section (<0.5 mm width) precisely localizes depth of infiltrates, foreign bodies, and thinning.

### Specular Reflection
Align angle of incidence = angle of reflection to visualize endothelial mosaic and lens shagreen.

### Retroillumination
Bounce light off iris or fundus to detect corneal neovascularization, microcysts, cataracts, and iris transillumination defects.

### Sclerotic Scatter
Decouple slit lamp and beam onto limbus; total internal reflection highlights subtle corneal opacities and stromal edema.

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Link:** Sclerotic scatter reveals subtle central corneal clouding (CCC) caused by hard contact lens overwear and hypoxia.
