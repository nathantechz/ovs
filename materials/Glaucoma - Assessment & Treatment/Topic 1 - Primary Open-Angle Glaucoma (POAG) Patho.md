# Glaucoma: Assessment & Treatment - Topic 1: Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma (POAG) Pathophysiology & Management
## Doctor of Optometry &middot; Ocular Diseases Curriculum

---

## Overview & Objectives
Optic nerve head cupping, ISNT rule violations, laminar pore visibility, disk hemorrhages (Drance), and target IOP titration.

## Core Concepts & Mechanisms

### ISNT Rule
Normal neuroretinal rim width order: Inferior > Superior > Nasal > Temporal. Violation (thinning of inferior or superior rim) indicates early glaucomatous optic neuropathy.

### Drance Hemorrhage
Flame-shaped splinter hemorrhage at the disk margin indicating active disease progression and localized RNFL loss.

### Medical Monotherapy Algorithm
First-line: Prostaglandin analogs (Latanoprost, Bimatoprost) for 25-35% IOP reduction. Second-line: Beta-blockers (Timolol), Alpha-2 agonists (Brimonidine), or CAIs (Dorzolamide).

## Clinical Pearls & Practice Links
> **Clinical Link:** A single disc margin splinter hemorrhage warrants immediate reassessment of target IOP and treatment escalation.
