## 🎯 What is OLH?

**OLH (Optometry Learning Hub)** is the world's first comprehensive, free, professionally-designed optometry education platform. It's a global resource bringing world-class optometry education to every student and faculty member on Earth.

### In Numbers
- **42 Comprehensive Courses** (Bachelor, Master, Doctor programs)
- **123+ Professional Textbooks** (22 subject categories)
- **25+ University Programs** (12+ countries)
- **600+ Study Materials** (lectures, notes, practicals)
- **6 Curriculum Standards** (country-specific)
- **1 Website** that works perfectly on all devices
- **0 Cost** - Completely free, no ads, no subscriptions
